What Routina App Does

Routina App lets you create daily, weekly, or custom habits and track your consistency over time. Every completed habit builds your streak, and our AI analytics feature (powered by Google Gemini) reviews your patterns to give you personalized suggestions for staying on track.

Habit TrackingCreate and log daily habits with custom frequency and reminders.
Streaks & ProgressVisualize your consistency with streak counters and completion rates.
AI InsightsGet personalized analysis of your habit patterns and suggestions.
Arabic & EnglishFull bilingual support with right-to-left layout for Arabic.
